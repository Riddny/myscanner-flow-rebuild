# Growth Audit — My Scanner

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/myscanner-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic My Scanner already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in the app's own design. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the quarterly plan when they close — 44,99 € every 3 months, no trial | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask what they need it for — then show their own toolkit | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Put their job in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Put a customer's words next to the button | CR +5–10% |
| 05 | Read their answers back while it sets up | CR +10–15% |

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from My Scanner's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for My Scanner.
